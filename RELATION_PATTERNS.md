# CEOS Relation Patterns

## Ontological level

A Relation Pattern is neither a material object nor an individual Observer. It is the reproducible organization of agreement and disagreement among multiple Observers or assignment channels.

```text
Object
Observer
Family
Relation Pattern
Admission State
```

These entities occupy different axes and must not be collapsed.

## Relation Pattern versus Admission State

```text
Admission State
    answers: May a claim be made?

Relation Pattern
    answers: How are independent channels organized relative to one another?
```

For LiMnVF₆:

```text
Admission State:
    BOUNDARY

Relation Pattern:
    RELATIONAL_BOUNDARY

Composite Knowledge State:
    BOUNDARY :: RELATIONAL_BOUNDARY
```

`RELATIONAL_BOUNDARY` is therefore not a replacement for `BOUNDARY` and not a peer of `OOD` in state precedence.

## Conceptual definition: RELATIONAL_BOUNDARY

> **RELATIONAL_BOUNDARY is a reproducible organizational configuration in which multiple independently constructed assignment channels remain valid under their own Builder contracts but support incompatible nearest organizational families.**

It is a property of concordance in the current descriptive language, not an intrinsic material label and not a physical phase claim.

The conceptual object precedes any particular threshold, neighborhood size or algorithm. Different operational versions may test the same concept, but their results must remain versioned and cannot be silently merged.

## Operational definition: RELATIONAL_BOUNDARY v0.3

An object has Relation Pattern `RELATIONAL_BOUNDARY` under protocol v0.3 when all conditions hold:

1. Builder Admission is valid.
2. Frozen preprocessing and exact replay are valid.
3. The global representation is in-support: distance percentile ≤ 99.
4. Global centroid margin is below 0.05.
5. Global and Local geometry support different nearest families.
6. At least two of Composition, Structure and Symmetry support a family different from Global.

Channel-specific OOD is retained as a node attribute and does not automatically invalidate this pattern. Global OOD has precedence and changes the Assignment State to `OOD`.

## Meaning of PASS

```text
Population Support: PASS
```

means:

> The organizational relation pattern is supported in the analyzed sample, under the frozen representation, registered channel definitions, neighborhood contract, thresholds and Conflict Nulls.

It does **not** mean:

- evidence of a physical mechanism;
- evidence of a thermodynamic or quantum phase boundary;
- validation of a classical material category;
- universal validity outside the analyzed WORLD.

## Required wording

Every population-support report for a Relation Pattern must include:

```text
PASS supports existence of the organizational pattern
within the analyzed sample and registered methodology.
No physical phase-boundary or mechanism claim is made.
```
