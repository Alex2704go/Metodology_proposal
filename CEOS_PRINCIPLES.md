# CEOS Principles

## Principle 1 — Ontology–Vocabulary Separation

External scientific terms are vocabulary entities. They may be mapped to CEOS entities but do not define them.

```text
External Term ≠ CEOS Object
```

## Principle 2 — Cross Mapping Asymmetry

> **Vocabulary correspondence ≠ evidence inheritance.**

Cross Mapping translates between vocabularies. It does not transfer evidence in either direction.

## Principle 3 — Descriptive Mapping

```text
Cross Mapping is descriptive.
It never upgrades the epistemic status of either ontology.
Only independently admitted evidence may upgrade epistemic status.
```

## Principle 4 — Nearest Is Not Admissible

```text
nearest_family ≠ admissible_family
```

Geometric proximity is not an admitted membership claim.

## Principle 5 — State–Pattern Separation

```text
Admission State ≠ Relation Pattern
```

Admission State qualifies permissible claims. Relation Pattern describes agreement and conflict among channels.

## Principle 6 — Mapping–Evidence Separation

```text
Mapping Strength ≠ Evidence Strength
```

A structurally strong correspondence can have no independently admitted physical evidence.

## Principle 7 — Provenance Is Part of the Claim

A claim is not fully specified without the artifacts, transformations, protocols and prior claims from which it was constructed.

## Principle 8 — Explicit Interpretation Boundary

Every claim-bearing CEOS document states what it supports and what it does not support. Absence of a prohibition is not permission.

## Principle 9 — Independent Admission for Claim Upgrade

Every transition to a stronger claim type requires new evidence and its own Admission. Earlier evidence may be referenced but is not automatically inherited.

## Principle 10 — Versioned Non-Erasure

New failures, contradictions and qualifications do not rewrite earlier maturity history. They create a new version and may trigger `REASSESSMENT_REQUIRED`.

## Engineering analogy

```text
Well-typed research does not guarantee truth.
It rejects identifiable classes of invalid inference.
```

## Interpretation Boundary — Document Scope

### Supports

- the protocol, vocabulary, decision history, or document-governance contract explicitly stated here; empirical claims only through separately admitted linked artifacts.

### Does not support

- physical mechanism;
- thermodynamic phase;
- microscopic Hamiltonian;
- causal explanation;
- prediction of untested properties;
- transfer of evidence through vocabulary or Cross Mapping;
- universal validity outside the registered WORLD, sample, protocol, and version;
- empirical validation merely because a rule is documented.

### Cross Mapping Asymmetry

> **Vocabulary correspondence ≠ evidence inheritance.**
