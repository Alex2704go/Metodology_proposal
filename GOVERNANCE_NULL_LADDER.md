# Governance Null Ladder

## Purpose

Governance Null Ladder attacks the construction, provenance, lifecycle and logical admissibility of a claim package rather than the underlying organizational data result.

```text
Input:
    claim package + proof objects + lifecycle + dependency graph

Attack target:
    validity of scientific reasoning and governance

Output:
    rejected invalid transition or registered specification gap
```

## Examples

- ghost evidence;
- ghost provenance;
- self-asserted transition evidence;
- unpinned antecedent;
- illegal lifecycle transition;
- maturity without qualification history;
- independence without registered identities;
- cyclic claim graph;
- semantic Supports/Prohibits contradiction;
- evidence inheritance across vocabulary mapping;
- claim-bearing artifact escaping the registry.

## Question answered

```text
Does this claim package have the right to exist
under the current CEOS specification?
```

It does not answer whether the claim is physically true.

## Engineering cycle

```text
Specification
    ↓
Governance Red Team
    ↓
Specification Gap Registry
    ↓
Revised Governance
    ↓
Regression on unchanged attacks
```

## Outcome terminology

- `SPECIFICATION_GAP`: invariant absent or under-specified;
- `UNPROTECTED_TRANSITION`: invalid transition not blocked;
- `IMPLEMENTATION_DEFECT`: implementation violates its specification;
- `REJECTED`: invalid candidate correctly blocked;
- `SECURITY_BREACH`: reserved for actual compromise of an admitted protected system.

## Scope rule

`Remaining registered gaps: 0` means only that every currently registered invalid attack was rejected. It never means that no unregistered vulnerability exists.

## Interpretation Boundary — Document Scope

### Supports

- the protocol, vocabulary, decision history, or document-governance contract explicitly stated here; empirical claims only through separately admitted linked artifacts.

### Does not support

- physical mechanism;
- thermodynamic phase;
- microscopic Hamiltonian;
- causal explanation;
- prediction of untested properties;
- transfer of evidence through vocabulary or Cross Mapping;
- universal validity outside the registered WORLD, sample, protocol, and version;
- empirical validation merely because a rule is documented.

### Cross Mapping Asymmetry

> **Vocabulary correspondence ≠ evidence inheritance.**
